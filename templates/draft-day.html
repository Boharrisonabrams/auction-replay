What this calculation proves

The budget ceiling is your budget minus all other players in Plan A. It is an affordability limit for that exact plan, not an optimal bid or a promise the remaining players will be available at those prices.

The projection comparison sums DS Proj for QB, RB×2, WR×2, TE, and FLEX. It does not model weekly lineup choices, bye coverage, injuries beyond the source forecast, waivers, opponents’ reactions, or win probability. No premium multiplier comes from historical outcomes. K/DEF projections are excluded because complete scoring parity is unverified.

The 10–30% stress applies to all future Plan B purchases and all future Plan A purchases except the current nominee. Owned prices stay fixed. Market reference prices and dynamic provider quotes are separate inputs.
