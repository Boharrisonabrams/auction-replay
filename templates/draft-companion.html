    What this comparison does and does not measure
    • Each step restores the actual historical prefix. Hypothetical purchases do not carry into the next nomination. These are separate decision comparisons, not a simulated alternate season.
    • Forecast pages display dates before the 2025 draft but were retrieved in 2026. They were not independently archived before the draft; source hashes establish reproducibility now.
    • Future prices combine remaining cash, undrafted values and earlier sale ratios, then apply the selected scenario. They are estimates, not guaranteed purchase opportunities.
    • The joint objective uses projected core-offense starting lineups in Weeks 1–17, including known byes. Season forecasts are distributed uniformly over 17 NFL games. K/DEF scoring and waiver management remain excluded; all future specialists cost $1.
    • Supported bids have a buy lower bound above the pass upper bound. Possible bids include unresolved comparisons and are not precise recommendations. A work limit can leave a finite upper bound without a feasible roster found.
    • The performance selector tests a lower forecast or two explicitly absent non-bye weeks; these are assumptions, not injury probabilities. Neutral ceilings and the sensitivity range stay visible.
    • Some late historical rosters cannot satisfy the model’s starter constraints. In those cases, cash capacity remains visible and the model abstains.
    • 2025 history alone does not establish a reliable manager behavior model or a proven scoring advantage.
